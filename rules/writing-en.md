# English writing rules

Every sentence you keep must add something the reader does not already have. Fix structure before words.

## Staging
- Cut "not X but Y" contrasts that only add weight, one-line closers that repeat the point, and run-ups before the point. State it.

## Rhythm by rule
- No forced triads, no repeated sentence openings, no dash as the universal connector. Vary length because the meaning asks for it.

## Inflation
- Ordinary facts stay ordinary. No "pivotal", "crucial", "testament", no borrowed authority ("experts say") without a named source. Say "is" and "has".

## Formatting by rule
- Bold only what a skimmer must not miss. Sentence-case headings. Straight quotes. No decorative emoji.

## Leftovers
- Remove chat wrappers ("Great question", "I hope this helps"), knowledge-limit disclaimers, and headings repeated in the first sentence.

## Wrong reader
- Do not re-explain what the reader already knows from the conversation. Put the decision first.

## When not to act
- Leave quotes, code, numbers, names and the author's deliberate style alone. If a sentence is plain and true, keep it.
